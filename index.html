Spice Island Escape

Zanzibar Beach Holidays

After the dust of the safari, sink your feet into Zanzibar's powder-white sand. The island's turquoise Indian Ocean waters, rich Swahili culture, legendary seafood and vibrant coral reefs make it the perfect grand finale to any Tanzania adventure.

Pristine White-Sand Beaches
Nungwi, Kendwa, Paje, Matemwe — some of the Indian Ocean's most breathtaking shores lined with boutique resorts and beach bars.
Stone Town – UNESCO World Heritage
Wander the labyrinthine alleys of the ancient Arab trading port with over 2,000 years of layered history, culture and architecture.
Spice Farm Tours
Once the world's leading clove producer, Zanzibar's fertile soil also grows vanilla, cinnamon, nutmeg, black pepper and more.
Diving, Snorkelling & Marine Life
World-class coral reefs, sea turtles, whale sharks and vibrant marine life in the warm crystal-clear Indian Ocean waters.
