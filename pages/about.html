Who We Are

Tanzania's Locally Owned Adventure Outfitter

We are a locally owned tour company with a quality-first approach to offer the best possible Tanzanian safaris, Mount Kilimanjaro climbs, Zanzibar beach holidays, and much more. Based in Arusha — the safari capital of Africa — our team has grown into a tight-knit family dedicated to making your dream journey a reality.

We believe every journey should be transformative. We've spent years crafting unforgettable safari experiences that connect travellers with the raw beauty, magnificent wildlife, and rich cultures of East Africa. Our passionate team of local guides, skilled cooks and dedicated travel specialists are devoted to sharing their deep knowledge of the land and its people.

The People Behind Your Safari

Meet Our Team

Every role in our team exists for one purpose: to make your experience seamless, safe and unforgettable.

🧭
Driver-Guides
The heart of your safari. They lead with expertise, track wildlife through experience, share stories about nature and culture, ensure your comfort and safety, and transform every game drive into lasting memories.
📋
Reservations Team
Working behind the scenes to ensure a seamless experience. They confirm all bookings — accommodations, flights, transfers and park entry — coordinate with lodges and communicate special needs in real-time.
👋
Briefing & Welcome Team
Your journey starts with them. A warm airport welcome, transfer to your hotel, a full itinerary walkthrough, safety guidelines, and every question answered. They prepare you for an unforgettable adventure.
🍽️
Safari Cooks
Skilled chefs who prepare fresh, hearty meals in the field — from bush picnics to camp dinners under the stars. Dietary requirements and special needs are always accommodated with a smile.
🏔️
Mountain Porters
The lifeblood of every Kilimanjaro expedition. Our certified TANAPA-licensed porters carry loads to strict weight limits, set up camps before your arrival and often welcome you with warm songs at each site.
💼
Operations & Sales
Our operations and sales specialists craft custom itineraries, handle all logistics and ensure every element of your trip — from first enquiry to final farewell — runs without a hitch.
🌿
Conservation Partners
We work closely with Tanzania National Parks (TANAPA) and the Ngorongoro Conservation Authority, supporting ethical wildlife management and anti-poaching initiatives across all parks we visit.
🤝
Community Liaisons
Connecting travellers with local Maasai communities, schools, and artisans in a respectful, meaningful way — ensuring tourism dollars flow directly to the people who call these landscapes home.
