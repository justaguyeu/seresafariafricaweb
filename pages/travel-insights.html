Visas & Entry
Tanzania Visa & Entry Requirements

Most nationalities require a visa to enter Tanzania. You can obtain a convenient e-Visa online through the official Tanzania Immigration portal before you travel, or get a visa on arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport. Current fee is approximately $50 USD. Yellow Fever certificate may be required depending on your country of origin.

Health & Safety
Health, Vaccinations & Safari Safety

Staying healthy on safari is straightforward with the right preparation. Consult your doctor 6–8 weeks before departure about malaria prophylaxis, recommended vaccinations (Hepatitis A & B, Typhoid, Tetanus), and any personal health considerations. Our guides carry comprehensive first aid kits and emergency contacts at all times.

Packing Guide
The Complete Tanzania Safari Packing List

Packing smart for a Tanzania safari means travelling light while covering every essential. Neutral-coloured clothing (khaki, olive, tan) is ideal for game drives. Layers are key — mornings can be cold, afternoons warm. A wide-brimmed hat, quality sunscreen, insect repellent with DEET, binoculars, and a good camera are all essentials. We provide a detailed packing guide upon booking.

Kilimanjaro
How to Train for a Kilimanjaro Climb

Kilimanjaro requires no technical climbing skills, but strong cardiovascular fitness significantly improves your summit success rate. We recommend 3–6 months of dedicated training — hiking with elevation gain, running, cycling, and strength work. "Pole, pole" (slowly, slowly) is the Kilimanjaro philosophy — pacing yourself correctly is more important than fitness alone.

Zanzibar
Zanzibar Travel Tips — What to Know Before You Go

Zanzibar is a predominantly Muslim island with a rich conservative culture — respectful dress when away from the beach is appreciated and expected. Bargaining is normal in local markets. The Swahili word "Karibu" (welcome) will greet you everywhere. Tipping, tides affecting beach access, and choosing the right area of the island for your travel style are all important factors to consider before you book.

Understanding Maasai Culture & Safari Etiquette

The Maasai people are one of East Africa's most iconic and culturally rich communities. Visiting a Maasai village is a highlight for many travellers — but doing so respectfully matters. Our guides help you navigate cultural encounters with sensitivity, from appropriate greetings and gift-giving to photography etiquette and understanding traditional customs and practices.

Money & Budget
Money, Currency & Tipping in Tanzania

The Tanzanian Shilling (TZS) is the local currency, but US Dollars are widely accepted for tourist services, souvenirs and tips. Bring clean, un-torn USD bills dated after 2009. ATMs in Arusha are reliable but can be scarce in parks. Standard safari tipping: Guide $15–20/day, Cook $10/day, Camp Staff $5/day per guest. Tips are greatly appreciated and make a real difference.

Photography
Safari Photography Tips — Capture the Perfect Shot

Tanzania is a photographer's paradise. Golden hour light (the hour after sunrise and before sunset) produces the most stunning wildlife images. A zoom lens of 300–600mm is ideal for wildlife. Always shoot in RAW format for maximum editing flexibility. Our guides position the vehicle for optimal light and angles — and know exactly when to be patient and when to move fast for the shot.

Tanzania Quick Facts

Essential information every traveller needs to know

Capital City
Dodoma (official) · Dar es Salaam (commercial) · Arusha (safari capital)
Languages
Swahili (official) & English. Our guides speak fluent English, French, Italian and other languages on request.
Electricity
230V / 50Hz. Type G sockets (UK three-pin). Bring a universal adapter. Lodges have charging points in rooms.
Time Zone
East Africa Time (EAT) — UTC+3. No daylight saving. 3 hours ahead of London, 8 ahead of New York.
Currency
Tanzanian Shilling (TZS). USD widely accepted. Bring clean, post-2009 USD bills. ATMs available in Arusha.
Connectivity
Good mobile coverage in Arusha and towns. Limited WiFi in remote parks. Lodges typically have WiFi in common areas.
Climate
Tropical. Dry season (Jun–Oct) averages 20–28°C. Nights in Ngorongoro can drop to 5°C. Zanzibar stays 26–32°C year-round.
Main Airports
Kilimanjaro International (JRO) — closest to Arusha & Northern Circuit. Julius Nyerere (DAR) — Dar es Salaam. Abeid Amani Karume (ZNZ) — Zanzibar.
