Tour Overview

This 4-day fly-in safari is designed for travellers already enjoying a beach holiday in Zanzibar who want to add Tanzania's finest wildlife experience without the long road journey. A scenic 35-minute flight from Zanzibar airport whisks you directly to the Serengeti airstrip — no long drives, no wasted time. You land in the heart of the world's greatest wildlife arena.

Over 4 unforgettable days you'll experience two full days of game driving in the Central Serengeti and a complete day inside the Ngorongoro Crater — Africa's most extraordinary natural enclosure. Big cats, the Big Five, the ancient caldera landscape and the endless golden savannah all await. A return flight brings you back to Zanzibar or connects you onward.

This is the perfect "beach plus bush" combination — you see the best of Tanzania's wildlife without losing a single day of your Zanzibar holiday to road travel.

Day-by-Day Itinerary
Day
1
Zanzibar → Serengeti (Fly-In) → Afternoon Game Drive
Zanzibar Airport → 35-min scenic flight → Seronera Airstrip (Serengeti) → Game Drive

Transfer from your Zanzibar hotel to Zanzibar International Airport (ZNZ) for your scheduled morning flight to the Serengeti. The 35-minute scenic flight over Tanzania's coast, then the Masai Steppe and into the Serengeti is spectacular — keep your camera ready. Land at Seronera Airstrip in the heart of Central Serengeti, where your driver-guide and safari vehicle await on the tarmac. Begin your first game drive immediately — you are already inside one of Africa's greatest wildlife sanctuaries. Afternoon game drives through the Seronera Valley, famous for its resident leopards, large lion prides and the year-round Seronera River wildlife. Arrive at camp before sunset for dinner and your first night under the stars.

Scenic Flight from Zanzibar Immediate Game Drive on Landing Leopard Search — Seronera Valley Seronera River Drive
Night 1: Serengeti Kati Kati Tented Camp OR Serengeti Heritage Camp (inside NP) | Dinner & Breakfast
Day
2
Full Day — Central Serengeti National Park
Pre-Dawn Departure → Morning Game Drive → Bush Lunch → Afternoon Drive → Camp Sunset

A full, dedicated day exploring the Serengeti. Rise before 6:00 AM as the horizon turns amber — the best predator activity happens in the cool early morning. Your guide navigates across the plains following tracks, radio calls from other guides, and years of field knowledge. The Central Serengeti is renowned for its resident lion coalitions (including large male groups), cheetah families, spotted hyena clans, leopard sightings in fever trees along the river, and enormous herds of wildebeest and zebra. Visit the Moru Kopjes — ancient granite outcrops home to rock hyrax, klipspringer and resting lions. Lunch is a packed picnic in the field. The afternoon drive explores different sections of the park. Return to camp in golden-hour light for dinner and stargazing.

Pre-Dawn Departure Lion Prides & Cheetah Moru Kopjes Exploration Bush Picnic Lunch Leopard in Fever Trees Serengeti Stargazing
Night 2: Serengeti Kati Kati Tented Camp OR Serengeti Heritage Camp | Full Board
Day
3
Serengeti → Ngorongoro Crater Floor — Full Day
Early Drive to Ngorongoro → Descent → Full Crater Day → Ascent → Crater Rim Lodge

Depart the Serengeti at dawn and drive approximately 2 hours to the Ngorongoro Conservation Area. Descend from the crater rim (2,300m) into the ancient caldera — 600 metres straight down on a winding track, with magnificent views expanding as you drop. The crater floor (260 km²) is one of the densest wildlife areas on the planet. Year-round residents include: large lion prides, spotted hyenas denning on the crater wall, the critically endangered black rhino (up to 30 individuals live here), enormous elephant bulls, Cape buffalo herds of thousands, hippos in the Gorigor Swamp, golden jackals, serval cats, bat-eared foxes, and over 200 bird species including vast flocks of lesser flamingo. Enjoy a picnic lunch beside the hippo pool. Full afternoon on the crater floor before ascending at dusk to your crater-rim lodge for the night.

Early Crater Descent All Big Five — Including Black Rhino Gorigor Swamp Hippo Pool Lesser Flamingo Flocks Crater Picnic Lunch Rim Sunset Views
Night 3: Ngorongoro Marera Mountain View Lodge OR Ngorongoro Wildlife Lodge (Crater Rim) | Full Board
Day
4
Ngorongoro → Arusha Airport → Onward Flight
Breakfast → Scenic Drive → Arusha → KIA Airport or Direct Return Flight to Zanzibar

After a final breakfast on the crater rim with panoramic morning views across the caldera, depart for Arusha. The drive through the Ngorongoro highlands takes approximately 2.5 hours, passing Maasai villages, coffee farms and the dramatic Rift Valley escarpment. Arrive at Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) for your onward international flight, or transfer to Arusha Airport for a direct return flight to Zanzibar. For guests continuing their Zanzibar holiday, a short scenic flight returns you to ZNZ by midday — your Zanzibar hotel check-in is easy to arrange. Our team assists with all luggage and departure logistics.

Final Crater Rim Panorama Highland Scenic Drive KIA or Arusha Airport Transfer Option: Return Flight to Zanzibar
End of Safari: Airport transfer to KIA, Arusha Airport, or return flight to Zanzibar

Important Information

Flight logistics: Flights are scheduled shared charters operating Zanzibar–Seronera (Serengeti). Flight times are approximately 7:30–8:30 AM from Zanzibar. We coordinate your Zanzibar hotel pickup accordingly. Luggage limit on bush flights is 15kg per person in a soft bag.

Luggage: Bush aircraft have strict luggage limits. Maximum 15kg in a soft duffel bag (no hard-shell suitcases). A luggage storage service is available at your Zanzibar hotel for items you don't need on safari.

Advance booking: Bush flight seats are limited and book up quickly, especially during peak season (June–October). We recommend booking at least 3 months in advance to secure your preferred dates and flight seats.

What to wear: Neutral safari colours (khaki, olive, tan). A warm fleece for the Ngorongoro crater rim nights (can drop to 5°C). Sunscreen, hat and sunglasses essential on the crater floor during midday.
