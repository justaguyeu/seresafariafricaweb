Tour Overview

This 3-day safari is Seresafari's most popular short package — a perfectly intense introduction to Tanzania's two greatest wildlife destinations. In just three days you will experience the world-famous Serengeti's endless plains and the extraordinary Ngorongoro Crater, Africa's greatest natural wildlife arena.

Staying in a mid-range tented camp inside Central Serengeti National Park puts you right in the heart of the action — game drives begin the moment you leave camp. Big cats, the Maasai people, massive herds of wildebeest and zebra, and the spectacular descent into the Ngorongoro Crater make this a safari that punches far above its duration.

This tour is ideal for travellers with limited time who want to experience the absolute best of Tanzania without compromise. It is also a perfect combination with a Zanzibar beach extension.

Day-by-Day Itinerary
Day
1
Arusha → Central Serengeti National Park
Arusha → Ngorongoro Highlands → Naabi Hill Gate → Serengeti (6 hrs total)

Early morning departure from Arusha — typically 6:00–6:30 AM. Your driver-guide collects you from your hotel and you begin the scenic drive northwest through the lush Arusha landscape, rising into the Ngorongoro Highlands and passing Maasai villages, cattle herds and breathtaking escarpment views. Cross into the Ngorongoro Conservation Area and descend toward the Serengeti, entering at Naabi Hill Gate. An immediate afternoon game drive through the rolling Central Serengeti plains follows — the vast savannah stretching to every horizon is immediately awe-inspiring. Arrive at your tented camp before sunset for dinner under the African sky.

Afternoon Game Drive Ngorongoro Highland Drive Maasai Villages en Route First Wildlife Sightings
Night 1: Serengeti Kati Kati Tented Camp OR Serengeti Heritage Tented Camp (inside Serengeti NP) | Dinner & Breakfast
Day
2
Full Day — Central Serengeti National Park
Sunrise Game Drive → Bush Picnic → Afternoon Game Drive → Sundowner at Camp

A full, uninterrupted day exploring the Central Serengeti — often considered the finest game-viewing area in all of Africa. Wake before dawn and depart camp by 6:00 AM as the golden light floods the plains. The Central Serengeti is home to multiple resident lion prides, large cheetah coalitions, leopards resting in acacia trees, enormous elephant families, giraffe silhouetted against the sunrise, and countless zebra and wildebeest herds. Your guide follows fresh tracks and ranger radio reports to position the vehicle for optimal sightings. After a bush picnic lunch, the afternoon game drive continues into different areas of the park — the Moru Kopjes, Seronera River, and Retina Hippo Pool are all within easy reach. Return to camp at sunset for a hot shower, dinner and stargazing.

Sunrise Game Drive Lion & Cheetah Search Retina Hippo Pool Bush Picnic Lunch Leopard Spotting Sundowner at Camp
Night 2: Serengeti Kati Kati Tented Camp OR Serengeti Heritage Tented Camp | Full Board
Day
3
Serengeti → Ngorongoro Crater → Arusha
Early Departure → Crater Descent → Full-Day Crater Floor → Ascent → Arusha

Rise before dawn for the drive from Serengeti to the Ngorongoro Crater rim, arriving in time for an early morning descent. The crater descent road winds steeply down 600 metres into Africa's greatest natural arena. The crater floor (260 km²) supports over 25,000 mammals in a self-contained ecosystem — lions, spotted hyena, black rhino, elephant, Cape buffalo, hippo, golden jackals, bat-eared fox, flamingo and hundreds of bird species all inhabit this ancient caldera year-round. Your guide works the crater thoroughly — from the hippo pool at Gorigor Swamp to the black rhino territory in the western grasslands. Enjoy a packed lunch at the designated picnic area. Late afternoon ascent from the crater and drive back to Arusha, arriving in the evening for hotel or airport drop-off.

Important Information

Early starts: Game drives depart before sunrise (typically 5:30–6:00 AM). This is when big cats are most active and the light is most spectacular for photography. Guides will wake guests in advance with hot tea or coffee.

Drive times: The drive from Arusha to the Serengeti takes approximately 5.5–6 hours including park entry. An early start from Arusha (6:00–6:30 AM) is essential to maximise time on the Serengeti by afternoon.

Ngorongoro temperatures: The crater rim sits at 2,300m altitude. Nights can drop to 5–8°C. Bring a warm fleece or jacket. The crater floor is warmer but can be cool in the morning during the descent.

Combine with Zanzibar: This 3-day safari pairs perfectly with a Zanzibar beach extension. Fly from the Serengeti airstrip directly to Zanzibar airport (35-minute scenic flight). Ask about our combined packages.
