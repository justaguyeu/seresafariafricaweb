Bush to Beach — Safari & Zanzibar

7 Days / 6 Nights
2 – 8 Passengers
Safari + Scenic Flight + Zanzibar
Tarangire · Serengeti · Ngorongoro · Zanzibar
Tour Overview

The Bush to Beach is Seresafari's signature complete Tanzania experience — combining four extraordinary days on safari through the Northern Circuit with three nights of pure paradise on Zanzibar's white-sand beaches. This is Tanzania in its entirety: raw wildlife and ancient landscapes followed by warm turquoise waters, Swahili culture and spice-scented island evenings.

The safari portion covers Tarangire National Park, the Central Serengeti and the Ngorongoro Crater — Tanzania's three most iconic wildlife destinations. After the final safari game drive, a scenic 35-minute flight from the Serengeti airstrip whisks you to Zanzibar, where three nights at a hand-picked beach resort complete this unforgettable journey.

This is our most requested package for honeymooners, anniversary couples, and travellers who want the definitive East Africa adventure without compromise. Every detail is handled by our team from start to finish.

Day-by-Day Itinerary
Day
1
Arusha → Tarangire National Park
Arusha (1.5 hrs drive) → Tarangire Gate → Full Afternoon Game Drive

Your driver-guide collects you from your Arusha hotel after an early breakfast. Drive southwest to Tarangire National Park, entering by midmorning for a full afternoon of game driving. The Tarangire River corridor is one of Africa's greatest dry-season wildlife concentrations — elephant herds of 200–300 animals move along the banks, lions and leopards hunt in the acacia woodland, and over 550 bird species inhabit this remarkable park. The ancient baobab trees — some thousands of years old — create an otherworldly backdrop for game photography. Return to your lodge before sunset for dinner and an introduction to the African night sounds.

Full Afternoon Game Drive Massive Elephant Herds Ancient Baobab Forest 550+ Bird Species
Night 1: Tarangire Simba Lodge OR Tarangire Tented Camp (Mid-Range) | Dinner & Breakfast
Day
2
Tarangire → Central Serengeti National Park
Tarangire Morning Drive → Highland Crossing → Serengeti Entry → Afternoon Game Drive

Early morning game drive in Tarangire before departing for the Serengeti. The route crosses the Ngorongoro Conservation Area highlands — a dramatic landscape of misty forests, Maasai cattle and sweeping views. Enter the Serengeti through Naabi Hill Gate and begin an immediate afternoon game drive across the iconic Central plains. Arrive at your tented camp inside the park before sunset — the first of two nights sleeping within this extraordinary national park.

Tarangire Sunrise Drive Ngorongoro Highland Crossing First Serengeti Game Drive Lion Prides at Sunset
Night 2: Serengeti Kati Kati Tented Camp (inside Serengeti NP, Mid-Range) | Full Board
Day
3
Full Day — Central Serengeti National Park
Pre-Dawn Departure → Morning Game Drive → Bush Picnic → Afternoon Game Drive → Sundowner

The Serengeti at its finest — a complete day with no destination, no schedule, just wildlife. Depart camp before 6:00 AM as golden light floods the savannah. Your guide navigates by fresh tracks and the morning radio network of sightings. Cheetah coalitions using termite mounds as vantage points, lions draped over kopje rocks, leopards with kills in acacia trees, elephants at the Seronera River, enormous giraffe herds moving through the open plain. After a field picnic, the afternoon drive continues — following the animals wherever they lead. Return to camp for a hot shower, dinner under an impossibly bright Serengeti sky, and the sound of distant lions.

Pre-Dawn Game Drive Cheetah Tracking Kopje Leopard Search Bush Picnic Lunch Optional Balloon Safari ($550) Serengeti Night Sky
Night 3: Serengeti Kati Kati Tented Camp | Full Board
Day
4
Serengeti → Ngorongoro Crater → Crater Rim Lodge
Morning Drive → Exit Serengeti → Olduvai Gorge → Ngorongoro Rim

A final morning game drive in the Serengeti before departing east toward Ngorongoro. Stop at Olduvai Gorge — the "Cradle of Mankind" where Mary Leakey discovered hominid fossils dating back 1.8 million years. A short guided museum walk brings this extraordinary human history to life. Continue to the Ngorongoro Crater rim (2,300m) for sunset views across the caldera from your luxury rim lodge. Tonight you dine with one of Africa's greatest natural wonders spread below your window.

Final Serengeti Game Drive Olduvai Gorge Museum Ngorongoro Rim Arrival Caldera Sunset Views
Night 4: Ngorongoro Marera Mountain View Lodge OR Ngorongoro Wildlife Lodge (Luxury, Crater Rim) | Full Board
Day
5
Ngorongoro Crater Floor → Serengeti Airstrip → Zanzibar
Dawn Crater Descent → Full Crater Day → Ascent → Drive to Airstrip → Flight to Zanzibar

The most dramatic day of the entire trip — beginning inside Africa's greatest natural arena and ending on a tropical island. Descend into Ngorongoro Crater at first light for a half-day game drive covering the hippo pool, black rhino territory, lion prides and flamingo-lined lake. After picnic lunch, ascend from the crater and drive to the Serengeti airstrip for your scheduled afternoon bush flight to Zanzibar. The 35-minute flight over Tanzania's interior is spectacular — watch the landscape shift from savannah to coast. Land at Zanzibar International Airport and transfer to your beach resort just in time for a sunset cocktail on the Indian Ocean. The contrast from dust and wildlife to sand and turquoise water is one of Tanzania's greatest travel experiences.

Ngorongoro Dawn Descent Big Five & Black Rhino Scenic Bush Flight to Zanzibar Zanzibar Beach Arrival Sunset on the Indian Ocean
Night 5: Zanzibar Beach Resort — Nungwi, Kendwa or Matemwe (Mid-Range/Luxury, your choice) | Breakfast & Dinner
Day
6
Zanzibar — Stone Town Tour & Free Beach Time
Morning Stone Town Tour → Afternoon Free Time on the Beach → Sunset Dhow Cruise

A morning guided walk through Stone Town — Zanzibar's UNESCO World Heritage Centre. Your local guide leads you through the labyrinthine alleys past carved doorways, spice markets, the former Slave Market memorial and the House of Wonders. Visit Freddie Mercury's childhood home and sample fresh Zanzibar street food at the Forodhani Gardens. Return to your resort by midday for a relaxed afternoon — swimming, snorkelling from the beach, a spa treatment, or simply doing nothing. As the sun descends, board a traditional dhow for a sunset cruise along Zanzibar's coast — one of the most romantic experiences the island offers.

Stone Town Guided Walk Slave Market Memorial Free Beach Afternoon Optional Snorkelling Sunset Dhow Cruise
Night 6: Zanzibar Beach Resort (same property) | Breakfast included
Day
7
Zanzibar — Free Morning → International Departure
Leisure Morning → Hotel Check-Out → Airport Transfer → International Flight

A leisurely final morning at your beach resort — one last swim, breakfast by the ocean, and time to pack your memories. Check out is typically midday; your transfer to Zanzibar International Airport (ZNZ) is arranged to meet your international flight departure. We recommend booking flights from Zanzibar to allow yourself a full morning on the island before departure. Our team can assist with any last-minute arrangements, luggage storage, or extended stays if you'd like to add extra nights in Zanzibar.

Final Beach Breakfast Last Morning Swim ZNZ Airport Transfer Safari Memories Forever
End of Journey: Transfer to Zanzibar International Airport for your onward flight

Important Information

Bush flight luggage limit: The Serengeti to Zanzibar flight has a strict 15kg soft bag limit per person. Leave hard-shell suitcases in Arusha storage. We provide luggage storage at no extra cost for items not needed on safari.

Advance booking essential: Bush flight seats between Serengeti and Zanzibar are extremely limited and book months in advance during peak season (June–October). We recommend booking at least 3–4 months ahead for this package.

Perfect for honeymoons: This is our most popular honeymoon package. Special touches — rose petals, champagne, private beach dinner — can be arranged at the Zanzibar resort. Please inform us at booking and we'll coordinate with the hotel.

Packing for both: Pack safari neutrals (khaki, olive) for days 1–5. Zanzibar is casual and warm — light cottons, swimwear and a light cover-up for Stone Town are all you need. A warm layer for Ngorongoro crater-rim nights is essential.
