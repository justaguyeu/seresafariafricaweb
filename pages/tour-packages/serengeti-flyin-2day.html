Tour Overview

The Mid-Range Serengeti Fly-In Safari is the most time-efficient way to experience Africa's greatest wildlife arena. Instead of spending an entire day driving from Arusha to the Serengeti (5–6 hours each way), a 45-minute scenic flight drops you directly at Seronera Airstrip in the heart of the Central Serengeti — saving you 10+ hours of road travel and maximising every moment with the wildlife.

This 2-day package is ideal for travellers on tight schedules who refuse to compromise on wildlife quality. You land in the Central Serengeti and begin game driving immediately. One full day of game drives inside the park. Return flight the following morning. Simple, efficient, extraordinary.

Despite its compact size, this tour delivers genuine Serengeti experiences — lions, cheetah, leopard, elephant, giraffe, wildebeest, zebra and the full cast of the world's greatest wildlife show. Mid-range tented camp accommodation inside the national park means game drives start from your doorstep.

Day-by-Day Itinerary
Day
1
Arusha → Serengeti (Scenic Flight) → Full Day Game Drives
Arusha Airport → 45-min scenic flight → Seronera Airstrip (Serengeti) → Game Drives

Transfer from your Arusha hotel to Arusha Airport for your scheduled morning departure. The 45-minute scenic flight to the Serengeti is an experience in itself — the aircraft climbs over Arusha's highland landscape, crosses the Masai Steppe and descends toward the vast golden plains of the Serengeti. Land at Seronera Airstrip and meet your driver-guide and 4WD Land Cruiser, waiting on the grass strip. Begin your first game drive immediately — you are in the heart of one of Africa's greatest national parks from the moment you step off the plane.

The Central Serengeti's Seronera Valley is famous for its year-round wildlife concentration — resident lion prides, large cheetah families, leopards in fever trees along the river, hippos and crocodiles in the Seronera River pools, giraffe and elephant families on the open plains. Afternoon game drive continues with fresh sightings reported via the guide radio network. Arrive at your tented camp before sunset for a hot shower, dinner and the sound of distant lions calling across the plains.

Night 1: Serengeti Kati Kati Tented Camp OR Serengeti Heritage Tented Camp (inside Serengeti NP, Mid-Range) | Dinner & Breakfast included
Day
2
Serengeti — Morning Game Drive → Return Flight to Arusha
Pre-Dawn Game Drive → Seronera Airstrip → 45-min return flight → Arusha

Rise before dawn for one final golden-hour game drive — the best time for predator activity. Your guide leads you into the Seronera Valley as the light turns amber and the plains come alive. Lions returning from night hunts, cheetah mothers with cubs on termite mounds, enormous elephant families moving toward the river, zebra and wildebeest herds stretching to the horizon in every direction. Kilimanjaro is sometimes visible on the eastern horizon on clear mornings — a breathtaking backdrop to the world's greatest wildlife show.

Return to Seronera Airstrip by mid-morning for your return scenic flight to Arusha. Land at Arusha Airport before midday. Transfer to your hotel or directly to Kilimanjaro International Airport for onward connections. For those combining with a Zanzibar extension, flights from Arusha to Zanzibar depart throughout the afternoon — we handle all coordination.

Important Information

Luggage limit: Bush aircraft on the Arusha–Serengeti route have a maximum luggage allowance of 15kg per person in a soft bag (no hard-shell suitcases). Store excess luggage at your Arusha hotel — we provide this service at no charge.

Book in advance: Scheduled bush flights fill up quickly, especially in peak season (June–October). We strongly recommend booking at least 6–8 weeks in advance to secure your preferred dates. Last-minute availability is limited.

Perfect Zanzibar combination: This 2-day fly-in pairs perfectly with a Zanzibar beach holiday. Arrive in Arusha, fly to Serengeti, return to Arusha, then connect to Zanzibar — all in 3 days. Ask us about combined packages.

Optional balloon safari: A hot air balloon safari over the Serengeti at sunrise (USD 550/person) can be added to Day 1 or Day 2. Balloons depart at first light and must be booked at least 3 weeks in advance. Includes a champagne bush breakfast after landing.
