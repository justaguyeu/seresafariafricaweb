Tour Overview

The Tanzania Classic Safari is our flagship itinerary — a perfectly balanced 6-day journey through Tanzania's four most iconic national parks and conservation areas. Beginning and ending in Arusha, this safari takes you from the elephant-rich baobab landscape of Tarangire to the endless plains of the Serengeti, down into the world-famous Ngorongoro Crater, and along the flamingo-lined shores of Lake Manyara.

This tour delivers the very best of Tanzania's Northern Safari Circuit. You will have two game drives each full day, maximising your time in the field. Accommodation ranges from comfortable tented camps inside the national parks to luxury lodges on the crater rim — all carefully selected for their wildlife position, quality and character.

Whether you are watching wildebeest cross a river in the Serengeti, sitting inside Africa's greatest natural arena at dawn, or tracking a lion pride through Tarangire's golden grass — this is Tanzania at its most extraordinary. Our expert driver-guides bring every landscape to life.

Day-by-Day Itinerary
Day
1
Arusha → Tarangire National Park
Arusha (1.5 hrs drive) → Tarangire Gate → Game Drive → Camp

After an early morning briefing at your Arusha hotel, your driver-guide collects you and you depart for Tarangire National Park. Upon entering the park you immediately begin your first game drive. Tarangire's dry-season landscape concentrates wildlife around the permanent river — you will encounter massive elephant herds (often 200–300 strong), giraffe, zebra, wildebeest, buffalo, impala, and the park's iconic ancient baobab trees. Afternoon game drive continues until sunset, when you retire to your tented camp for a hot dinner under the stars.

Game Drive (Full Day) Elephant Herds Baobab Forest Big Five Search
Night 1: Tarangire Simba Lodge / Tarangire Tented Camp (Mid-Range) | Dinner & Breakfast included
Day
2
Tarangire → Central Serengeti
Tarangire (exit) → Ngorongoro Highlands → Serengeti Gate (4.5 hrs) → Game Drive

After breakfast, depart Tarangire and drive through the breathtaking Ngorongoro highlands — a high-altitude landscape of Maasai cattle, lush forests and sweeping views. Cross the Ngorongoro Conservation Area boundary and descend toward the Serengeti. Enter through the Naabi Hill Gate and immediately begin your afternoon game drive across the iconic open plains. The Central Serengeti supports year-round resident lion prides, cheetah, leopard, giraffe and enormous herds of zebra and wildebeest. Arrive at your tented camp inside the park as the sun sets.

Afternoon Game Drive Lion Prides Cheetah Spotting Scenic Highland Drive
Night 2: Serengeti Kati Kati Tented Camp / Serengeti Wildebeest Camp (Mid-Range, Inside Park) | Full Board
Day
3
Full Day — Central Serengeti National Park
Morning Game Drive → Picnic Lunch → Afternoon Game Drive → Sundowner

A full day entirely devoted to exploring the world's most famous wildlife arena. Depart camp before dawn for a golden-hour game drive when predators are most active. The Central Serengeti's kopjes (rocky outcrops) are famous for lion groups and leopard sightings. After a packed lunch in the field, your guide takes you to different areas of the park in the afternoon — following fresh wildlife sightings reported by the ranger network. Optional hot-air balloon safari available at an additional cost (USD 550 per person, booked in advance). Return to camp for sundowner drinks and dinner.

Sunrise Game Drive Bush Picnic Lunch Predator Tracking Optional Balloon Safari Sundowner
Night 3: Serengeti Kati Kati Tented Camp / Serengeti Wildebeest Camp | Full Board
Day
4
Serengeti → Ngorongoro Crater Rim
Morning Game Drive → Exit Serengeti → Olduvai Gorge → Ngorongoro Crater Rim

Rise early for one final morning game drive in the Serengeti before departing toward Ngorongoro. En route, stop at the Olduvai Gorge Museum — one of the world's most important paleoanthropological sites where early hominid fossils dating back 1.8 million years were discovered. Continue to the Ngorongoro Crater rim (2,300m altitude) for spectacular aerial views down into the caldera. Check into your crater-rim lodge in time for sunset over the world's largest intact volcanic caldera — a truly breathtaking panorama.

Final Serengeti Game Drive Olduvai Gorge Visit Crater Rim Viewpoint Crater Sunset Views
Night 4: Ngorongoro Wildlife Lodge / Ngorongoro Marera Mountain View Lodge (Crater Rim) | Full Board
Day
5
Full Day — Ngorongoro Crater Floor
Descent at Sunrise → Full-Day Crater Floor Game Drive → Ascent at Dusk

The crown jewel of East African safaris. Descend 600 metres into Africa's greatest natural arena at first light, entering a self-contained world of over 25,000 mammals. The crater floor is one of the most densely populated wildlife areas on Earth — lions, spotted hyenas, black rhino (rare sighting), elephant, Cape buffalo, hippo, flamingos, zebra, wildebeest and over 500 bird species all live within the caldera year-round. Your guide knows every inch of the crater and will work hard to locate the black rhino, one of Africa's most endangered animals. Packed lunch enjoyed beside the hippo pool. Ascend before dark, arriving back at the rim lodge as the last light fades.

Dawn Crater Descent All Big Five (inc. Black Rhino) Hippo Pool 500+ Bird Species Bush Picnic Lunch
Night 5: Ngorongoro Wildlife Lodge / Ngorongoro Marera Mountain View Lodge | Full Board
Day
6
Lake Manyara National Park → Arusha (Departure)
Ngorongoro → Lake Manyara NP Game Drive → Arusha Hotel/Airport

After breakfast on the crater rim, descend into the Ngorongoro Conservation Area and drive toward Lake Manyara National Park for a final half-day game drive. Lake Manyara is famous for its tree-climbing lions — a behaviour unique to this park — as well as enormous elephant herds, large flocks of pink flamingos, hippos in the lake, and over 400 bird species. The park's groundwater forest along the base of the Rift Valley escarpment shelters colobus and vervet monkeys. After the game drive, depart for Arusha arriving in the late afternoon for hotel drop-off or airport transfer. Tonight you celebrate the end of your safari adventure.

Tree-Climbing Lions Flamingo Flocks Hippos in the Lake Departure Transfer

Important Information

Flights: The nearest airport is Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO), approximately 45 minutes from Arusha. We provide complimentary transfers from JRO or Arusha Airport. We recommend arriving the day before the safari starts.

Visa: Most nationalities can obtain a Tanzania e-Visa online at immigration.go.tz before departure. Fee is approximately $50 USD. Yellow Fever vaccination certificate may be required depending on your country of origin.

Health: Anti-malaria medication is strongly recommended. Consult your doctor at least 6 weeks before travel. Our guides carry comprehensive first aid kits and emergency oxygen on all game drives.

Insurance: Comprehensive travel insurance including medical evacuation cover is mandatory for all Seresafari guests. This is a non-negotiable requirement for your safety and our peace of mind.

What to Wear: Neutral colours (khaki, tan, olive, green) are recommended on game drives. Avoid blue and black clothing as these attract tsetse flies. Bring layers — mornings are cool, afternoons warm. A warm jacket is essential for Ngorongoro Crater rim nights.

Tipping: Tipping is customary and greatly appreciated. Recommended: Driver-Guide $15–20/day, Camp Staff $5–10/day per group. Tips are given directly to staff at the end of the safari.
