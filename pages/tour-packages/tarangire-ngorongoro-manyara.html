Tarangire, Ngorongoro & Lake Manyara

4 Days / 3 Nights
2 – 8 Passengers
Game Drives
Tarangire · Ngorongoro · Lake Manyara
Tour Overview

This 4-day safari explores three of Tanzania's most diverse and rewarding national parks without visiting the Serengeti — making it a slightly shorter drive and a great option for first-time Tanzania visitors or those combining with a Zanzibar beach holiday.

You'll experience the elephant paradise of Tarangire with its ancient baobab trees, the extraordinary flamingo-lined shores and tree-climbing lions of Lake Manyara, and the crown jewel of East Africa — the full-day descent into the Ngorongoro Crater. Accommodation includes a night at the acclaimed Ngorongoro Marera Mountain View Lodge on the crater rim — one of Tanzania's finest lodges with spectacular panoramic views.

This itinerary delivers an extraordinary diversity of landscapes, ecosystems and wildlife encounters across four compact but richly rewarding days.

Day-by-Day Itinerary
Day
1
Arusha → Tarangire National Park
Arusha (1.5 hrs) → Tarangire Gate → Full Afternoon Game Drive → Camp

Depart Arusha after breakfast for Tarangire National Park — Tanzania's "Elephant Playground." The 1.5-hour drive from Arusha brings you quickly into the park, where you begin your first game drive along the banks of the Tarangire River. During the dry season this river is a magnet for wildlife — you can expect enormous elephant herds (sometimes 200–300 strong), large buffalo herds, giraffe, zebra, wildebeest, oryx, eland, hartebeest, and numerous predators including lions. The park's ancient baobab trees create one of Africa's most distinctive landscapes. Afternoon game drive continues until sunset before returning to your lodge for dinner.

Full Afternoon Game Drive Massive Elephant Herds Ancient Baobab Forest Tarangire River Wildlife 550+ Bird Species
Night 1: Tarangire Simba Lodge OR Maramboi Tented Lodge (Mid-Range) | Dinner & Breakfast
Day
2
Tarangire → Lake Manyara National Park → Ngorongoro Rim
Tarangire → Lake Manyara Game Drive (half day) → Ngorongoro Crater Rim Lodge

After an early breakfast and a morning game drive in Tarangire, depart for Lake Manyara National Park — a 1-hour drive through the town of Mto wa Mbu. Lake Manyara is one of Tanzania's most diverse parks — a groundwater forest along the Rift Valley escarpment, open savannah, and the shallow alkaline lake itself. Search for the park's famous tree-climbing lions (unique to Manyara and the Serengeti), enormous elephant herds that use the forest, hippos wallowing in the lake, and vast flocks of lesser flamingo in pink clouds. The park hosts over 400 bird species — a birder's paradise. After the game drive, drive up the Rift Valley escarpment to the Ngorongoro Crater rim and check into your lodge with panoramic crater views at sunset.

Tree-Climbing Lions Lake Hippos 400+ Bird Species Pink Flamingo Flocks Rift Valley Escarpment Drive Crater Rim Sunset
Night 2: Ngorongoro Marera Mountain View Lodge OR Ngorongoro Wildlife Lodge (Crater Rim, Luxury) | Full Board
Day
3
Full Day — Ngorongoro Crater Floor
Dawn Descent → Full-Day Crater Floor Drive → Hippo Pool Lunch → Dusk Ascent

The undisputed highlight of the safari — a full day inside Africa's greatest natural arena. Descend 600 metres into the ancient caldera at first light, entering a self-contained ecosystem housing over 25,000 mammals. The crater floor (260 km²) supports extraordinary wildlife density: large lion prides hunting in the open grassland, spotted hyena clans near their dens on the crater wall, the critically endangered black rhino grazing in the western section, enormous elephant bulls, Cape buffalo herds thousands strong, golden jackals, serval cats, bat-eared foxes, warthogs, blue wildebeest, zebra, eland and flamingos wading in the soda lake. A bush picnic is enjoyed at the hippo pool — watching these enormous animals submerge and resurface just metres away. Full afternoon before ascending at dusk, arriving back at the rim lodge for dinner and a spectacular night sky.

Dawn Crater Descent All Big Five Black Rhino Search Hippo Pool Picnic Lunch Flamingos on the Soda Lake Night Sky Stargazing
Night 3: Ngorongoro Marera Mountain View Lodge OR Ngorongoro Wildlife Lodge | Full Board
Day
4
Ngorongoro → Arusha (Departure)
Final Rim Breakfast → Scenic Descent → Arusha Hotel or Airport (2.5 hrs)

Enjoy a leisurely final breakfast on the crater rim with a last panoramic sunrise view across the caldera. Depart mid-morning for the 2.5-hour drive back to Arusha through the Ngorongoro highlands and agricultural foothills. Your driver-guide provides a final briefing and will share safari photos taken during the trip. Arrive in Arusha by early afternoon for hotel drop-off or transfer to Kilimanjaro International Airport. This is the perfect point to add a Zanzibar beach extension — flights from Arusha to Zanzibar depart throughout the day.

Final Crater Rim Panorama Safari Photo Review with Guide Arusha / KIA Airport Transfer Optional Zanzibar Connection

Important Information

Ngorongoro altitude: The crater rim sits at 2,300m. Nights can be cold (5–8°C). Bring a warm jacket and fleece. The crater floor is warmer but breezy in the morning during descent. Layers are essential.

Best time for Tarangire: June–October is peak season when the Tarangire River acts as a magnet for wildlife. During the wet season (November–May) some animals disperse into the surrounding ecosystem, but birdlife is exceptional.

Lake Manyara tides: The water level and flamingo numbers at Lake Manyara vary with rainfall. Best flamingo sightings occur during and just after the rains (November–May). Tree-climbing lions are most easily spotted in the early morning.

Zanzibar connection: This tour pairs perfectly with a Zanzibar beach extension. Flights from Arusha to Zanzibar (ZNZ) operate daily. We can arrange the connection seamlessly — ask about our combined packages.
