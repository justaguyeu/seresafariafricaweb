Most Popular
Northern Tanzania UNESCO Heritage

Serengeti National Park

Tanzania's most celebrated park covers 14,763 km² of endless golden savannah, acacia woodland and rocky kopjes. Home to the world-famous Great Migration and all of the Big Five.

Best time: June–October & Jan–Feb (calving)
Wildlife: Wildebeest, lion, leopard, cheetah, elephant, buffalo, rhino
Experience: Great Migration, balloon safaris, game drives
Stay: Tented camps inside the park for best wildlife access
Explore Serengeti
Must Visit
Conservation Area UNESCO Heritage

Ngorongoro Crater

The world's largest intact volcanic caldera, 19 km across and 600 m deep, supporting over 25,000 mammals including one of Africa's densest lion populations and rare black rhino.

Best time: Year-round — wildlife density consistently exceptional
Wildlife: All Big Five including rare black rhino, flamingos
Experience: Full-day crater floor game drive, Maasai visits
Stay: Crater rim lodges with spectacular sunrise views
Explore Ngorongoro
Hidden Gem
Northern Tanzania Elephant Capital

Tarangire National Park

Tanzania's hidden gem — ancient baobab trees, seasonal swamps, and Africa's largest elephant herds (often 300+ in a single group) with far fewer tourists than the Serengeti.

Best time: June–October (dry season, peak wildlife concentration)
Wildlife: Elephants, lions, leopards, oryx, wildebeest, zebra
Experience: Baobab landscapes, walking safaris, night drives
Stay: Tented camps near the river for best elephant sightings
Explore Tarangire
Birdwatcher's Paradise
Rift Valley 400+ Bird Species

Lake Manyara

Compact but incredibly diverse — famous for tree-climbing lions, vast flamingo flocks, hippos, and over 400 bird species along the dramatic Rift Valley escarpment.

Best time: June–October; flamingos best Nov–June
Wildlife: Tree-climbing lions, flamingos, elephants, hippos
Experience: Birdwatching, forest drives, tree-climbing lion search
Stay: Lodges on the escarpment with panoramic lake views
Explore Lake Manyara

Turquoise Indian Ocean waters, powder-white beaches, UNESCO Stone Town, coral reefs sheltering sea turtles and whale sharks — the perfect safari extension.

Best Time to Visit Tanzania

Tanzania is a year-round destination. Each season offers unique wildlife experiences — here's how to choose your perfect timing.

June – October
Dry Season · Peak Safari

The best time for wildlife viewing. Animals congregate around water sources, vegetation is low for easier spotting, and the Great Migration river crossings happen in the Mara River (July–September).

November – December
Short Rains · Green Season

Short rains bring lush green landscapes and fewer crowds. Excellent birdwatching as migratory species arrive. Prices are lower and lodges less busy.

January – February
Calving Season · Predator Action

The Great Wildebeest Calving season unfolds on the Southern Serengeti. Up to 8,000 calves born each day, attracting an extraordinary concentration of predators.
