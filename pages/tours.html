Tanzania is a year-round destination. The dry season (June–October) offers the best wildlife viewing as animals gather around water sources. January–February is magical for the Great Migration calving season on the Southern Serengeti plains. November–December offers green landscapes and fewer crowds at lower prices.
Our standard safari vehicles accommodate a maximum of 6 guests, ensuring everyone has a window seat with unobstructed wildlife views. Private safaris can be arranged for couples, families or small groups in their own exclusive vehicle with a dedicated guide.
Most nationalities require a visa to enter Tanzania. You can apply for an e-Visa online before travel at immigration.go.tz, or obtain a visa on arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport. The current fee is approximately $50 USD for most nationalities. We recommend applying online in advance to avoid queues.
Yellow Fever vaccination is required if arriving from certain countries. We recommend consulting your doctor or a travel health clinic at least 6–8 weeks before travel. Malaria prophylaxis is strongly advised. Other recommended vaccines include Hepatitis A & B, Typhoid and Tetanus. Our team provides a detailed health briefing upon booking.
Our driver-guides are empowered to adapt your game drives based on fresh wildlife sightings, weather conditions, and your preferences. We always prioritise putting you in the best position to see remarkable wildlife. Major route changes require advance coordination with our operations team.
The Tanzanian Shilling (TZS) is the local currency, but US Dollars are widely accepted for most tourist services, tips, and souvenirs. We recommend bringing clean, un-torn USD bills dated after 2009 for the best exchange rates. ATMs are available in Arusha and major towns but may be unreliable in rural areas.
