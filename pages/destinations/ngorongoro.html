Overview

The Ngorongoro Crater is one of the most extraordinary places on Earth — a vast, collapsed volcanic caldera that has become a self-contained natural world teeming with life. Formed some three million years ago when a giant volcano imploded, the crater stretches 19 kilometres across and plunges 600 metres to its floor, creating a 304 km² natural enclosure that traps an astonishing diversity of wildlife year-round.

The crater floor supports over 25,000 large mammals — one of the densest concentrations of wildlife anywhere on the continent. Unlike most African parks where animals migrate with the seasons, the Ngorongoro's geography means its wildlife rarely needs to leave. This predictability, combined with the dramatic landscape of the crater walls rising around you on every side, makes for an utterly unique and memorable game viewing experience.

Most famously, the Ngorongoro is one of the best places on Earth to see all five members of Africa's Big Five in a single day: lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo, and the rare black rhino — which exists here in one of its last viable East African sanctuaries.

Crater Highlights

Black Rhino — Among Africa's rarest sightings. The crater protects one of Tanzania's last black rhino populations. Spot them in the open grassland near Lerai Forest.

Lion Prides — Ngorongoro has one of Africa's highest lion densities. Large prides of up to 40 animals roam the crater floor. Mating pairs, cubs, and hunts are common.

Lake Magadi — The soda lake at the crater's heart draws thousands of pink flamingos, pelicans, and wading birds into an unforgettable spectacle.

Lerai Forest — A magnificent fever tree forest inside the crater. Home to elephants, leopards, baboons, and waterbuck in dense green shade.

Maasai Settlements — The Maasai people live alongside wildlife in the Conservation Area. Boma village visits offer profound cultural insight.

Crater Rim Sunrise — Wake at your rim lodge, watch the clouds fill the crater below, then descend as the mist lifts to reveal the plains — breathtaking every single time.

Visiting Information

Game Drives: All vehicles must be 4WD. Only open roof vehicles permitted. Strict rules: no off-road driving, windows must remain closed near predators. Our guides know every kilometre.

Opening Hours: Crater entrance gates open at 06:00 and vehicles must exit by 18:00. Early descent (6:30am) gives you the crater in morning mist — pure magic.

Weather: The rim sits at 2,300m — it can be cold and misty. The crater floor is warmer. Pack a light jacket for morning descents and evening rim dinners.

Picnic Lunch: All guests eat packed lunches at designated hippo pool picnic site — with hippos wallowing metres away. No food may be left on vehicle roofs (kites will steal it immediately).
