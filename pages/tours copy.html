Tour FAQ

Everything you need to know before booking your Tanzania safari with Seresafari Africa.

Tanzania is a year-round destination. The dry season (June–October) offers the best wildlife viewing as animals gather around water sources. January–February is magical for the Great Migration calving season.
Our standard safari vehicles accommodate a maximum of 6 guests, ensuring everyone has a window seat with unobstructed wildlife views. Private safaris can be arranged for couples, families or small groups.
Most nationalities require a visa to enter Tanzania. You can apply for an e-Visa online before travel at immigration.go.tz, or obtain a visa on arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport. The current fee is approximately $50 USD.
Yellow Fever vaccination is required if arriving from certain countries. Malaria prophylaxis is strongly advised. Other recommended vaccines include Hepatitis A & B, Typhoid and Tetanus. Consult your doctor at least 6–8 weeks before travel.
The Tanzanian Shilling (TZS) is the local currency, but US Dollars are widely accepted. Bring clean, un-torn USD bills dated after 2009 for the best exchange rates. ATMs are available in Arusha and major towns.
