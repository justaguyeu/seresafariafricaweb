Why It Matters

Tourism That Transforms

We believe tourism should create meaningful benefits for the communities we work with. Every safari, every mountain climb, every beach extension you book with Seresafari Africa directly contributes to programmes that change lives here in Tanzania.

Tanzania is one of the most wildlife-rich nations on Earth, but many families — particularly those in rural areas near the parks — face significant hardship. Children lack access to basic school supplies. Orphans and vulnerable youth in care centres go without adequate nutrition, clothing and educational materials. Young people lack the skills to access employment.

We cannot operate in this landscape and turn a blind eye. This is not charity — it is responsibility. Tanzania gave us everything. We give back with every booking.

What We Do

Our Community Programmes

Four active programmes, each designed with local leaders, targeting real and urgent needs in the communities surrounding Tanzania's national parks and urban centres.

Education Access Programme

Many children in rural Tanzania walk hours to school, yet arrive without basic materials — no pen, no exercise book, no pencil. Our Education Access Programme provides comprehensive school kits to children from underserved families in communities around Arusha, Moshi and the Ngorongoro Conservation Area.

Each kit includes exercise books, pens, pencils, a ruler, an eraser, crayons, and a school bag. We partner directly with local primary school headteachers to identify the most vulnerable children and distribute kits at the start of every school term.

Active · 3 Regions

Orphan & Vulnerable Children Support

Tanzania has hundreds of thousands of orphaned children — watoto yatima — many living in care centres and residential homes run by religious organisations or community volunteers with very limited resources. These centres often struggle to provide adequate food, clothing, bedding and learning materials.

We visit partner orphanages and care centres across the Arusha and Kilimanjaro regions quarterly, bringing food parcels, clothing bundles, educational toys, stationery supplies, and toiletries. We also fund basic infrastructure repairs where most urgent.

Active · 8 Care Centres

Youth Empowerment & Skills Training

Young people who complete secondary school but cannot access university face a difficult gap. Through our Youth Empowerment Programme, we run vocational training workshops in partnership with local hospitality schools — covering guiding skills, English language, customer service, basic first aid, and wildlife ecology.

Graduates are supported into entry-level roles within Tanzania's tourism industry, creating a pipeline of locally trained professionals who stay in the community and contribute to its growth.

Active · 120+ Graduates

Women & Family Resilience

Women form the backbone of rural Tanzanian communities, yet often face the greatest economic hardship when breadwinners fall ill or families are disrupted. We support small groups of women — particularly single mothers and widows — through micro-grant funding for income-generating activities like crafts, vegetable farming, and small trade.

Guests who purchase locally made Maasai crafts and beadwork through Seresafari Africa directly fund these women's cooperatives. Nothing is marked up — every shilling goes back to the maker.

Active · 6 Women's Groups

Conservation & Environmental Education

Without thriving wildlife, there is no tourism. Without tourism, there is no community benefit. We close this loop by funding environmental education in schools near national parks — teaching children why healthy ecosystems matter, what coexistence with wildlife looks like, and how responsible tourism creates dignified livelihoods for their families.

We also co-fund anti-poaching community ranger patrols in buffer zones around Tarangire and the Ngorongoro Conservation Area, working directly with Tanzania National Parks Authority (TANAPA).

Active · TANAPA Partnership

Nutrition & Health Access

Malnutrition among young children in Tanzania's rural and peri-urban communities remains a serious challenge. We partner with local health volunteers (Mama Lishe — community nutrition volunteers) to deliver monthly food packages to families with severely malnourished children under five years of age.

In addition, we fund annual deworming and basic health screening days at partner primary schools, reaching children who otherwise would not access preventive healthcare. Healthy children learn better. Learning changes everything.

Active · Health Volunteers Network

Our Journey

Building Impact Year by Year

From a single school-kit drive to a structured multi-programme initiative — our community work has grown steadily alongside our safari business, because we believe one cannot thrive without the other.

Year 1 — The Beginning
First School Kit Distribution

Our founder personally delivered the first 40 school kits to a primary school in Arusha's outskirts. The smiles on those children's faces became the foundation of everything that followed.

Year 2 — Expanding Reach
First Orphanage Partnership

We formalised our first partnership with a children's home in the Kilimanjaro region, delivering quarterly visits with food, clothing and essential supplies to over 60 resident children.

Year 3 — Skills & Livelihoods
Youth Empowerment Launched

Our first vocational training cohort of 22 young people completed a 3-month tourism skills programme. Fifteen graduates secured employment in Tanzania's safari industry within six months.

Year 4 — Conservation Link
TANAPA Environmental Education Partnership

We signed a formal collaboration agreement with Tanzania National Parks to co-fund environmental education in five primary schools adjacent to Tarangire National Park.

Today — Ongoing
