Our Story

Our Foundation

Tanzania's Locally Owned Adventure Outfitter

Seresafari Africa is a proudly Tanzanian company — founded, owned, and led by a family that has called Arusha home for generations. From the very beginning, our promise has been simple: deliver authentic, unforgettable safari and cultural experiences with a relentless quality-first approach.

We believe every journey should be transformative. We have spent years crafting safari experiences that connect travellers with the raw beauty and magnificent wildlife of East Africa. Our passionate team of local guides, skilled cooks, and dedicated travel specialists has grown into a tight-knit family committed to one thing — making your dream journey a reality.

Mission, Vision & Values

The principles that guide every safari we operate, every guide we train, and every guest we welcome into Tanzania.

Our Mission

To provide ethical, memorable safaris in Tanzania while promoting conservation, cultural respect, community empowerment, and exceptional guest experiences that last a lifetime and inspire return visits to this extraordinary continent.

Our Vision

To be Tanzania's most trusted safari brand, inspiring global travellers through meaningful wildlife encounters and sustainable, culturally rich travel experiences that benefit local communities, conserve wildlife, and celebrate the natural world.

Our Values

Integrity, sustainability, safety, passion, respect, and local empowerment — the six values that shape every safari experience we offer across Tanzania's magnificent wild landscapes, from the Serengeti to Kilimanjaro's summit.

The People Behind the Magic

Our Dedicated Teams

From your first enquiry to your farewell transfer, every member of the Seresafari family plays a vital role in making your Tanzania adventure seamless, safe, and unforgettable.

Driver-Guides
The heart of your safari. Our experienced naturalist guides track wildlife through expertise, share stories about nature and culture, ensure your comfort and safety, and transform every game drive into vivid lasting memories filled with discovery and wonder.
Reservations Team
Working behind the scenes to ensure a seamless safari experience. They confirm all bookings — accommodations, flights, transfers, and park entry — coordinate with lodges and partners, and make sure your entire trip runs smoothly and stress-free from day one.
Briefing & Welcome Team
Our Briefing & Welcome Team ensures your journey starts smoothly. They greet you with a warm airport welcome, handle your hotel transfer, walk you through your full itinerary, share safety guidelines, and answer every question with clarity and warmth.
Safari Cooks
Our skilled chefs prepare delicious, nutritious meals in the most remote bush settings — from freshly cooked breakfasts before a morning game drive to elegant bush dinners under the stars, maintaining high standards of hygiene and taste throughout.
Mountain Porters
The unsung heroes of every Kilimanjaro climb. Our professional, licensed porters carry your gear, set up camps, and often welcome climbers into camp with songs. We restrict loads to ethical maximums and ensure fair, above-average wages for every porter.
Mountain Lead Guides
Each lead guide holds minimum 7 years' mountain experience, 150+ summits, TANAPA licensing, Wilderness First Responder certification, CPR certification, and fluent English. They monitor your health, set the pace, and communicate updates to our main office daily.
Sales & Planning Team
Our dedicated sales consultants work with you from the very first enquiry to craft a perfect itinerary matching your interests, schedule, and budget. They handle every detail — from route planning and lodge selection to special dietary needs and airport coordination.
Conservation Partners
We work hand-in-hand with national parks, conservation areas, and community-based organisations to ensure responsible tourism. A percentage of every booking supports local wildlife conservation projects and community development programmes across Tanzania.
